US Passport Photo

Starting camera…

    Stand 4 ft (1.2 m) in front of a plain white wall, face a window, and have someone else hold the phone at eye level.

    Drag to move, pinch to zoom & rotate. Fit the head (top of hair to chin) to the oval and keep the eyes inside the green band.

        Check these yourself

        • No glasses (take them off)
        • Neutral expression, mouth closed, both eyes open
        • No hat or head covering (unless religious/medical, with signed statement)
        • No headphones or wireless devices
        • Everyday clothes – no uniform or camouflage
        • Hair not covering eyes or face
        • Sharp, not blurry; taken within the last 6 months

        Print the 4×6 sheet at actual size (no “fit to page” or borders) on matte or glossy photo paper, then cut along the gray lines.

        US passport photo rules

        • Size: 2 × 2 in (51 × 51 mm), color
        • Head: 1 – 1 3/8 in (25 – 35 mm) from bottom of chin to top of head, including hair (50–69% of the photo)
        • Eyes: 1 1/8 – 1 3/8 in (28 – 35 mm) from the bottom of the photo
        • Background: plain white or off-white, no shadows or objects
        • Pose: face the camera directly, full face visible, head centered
        • Expression: neutral, mouth closed, both eyes open
        • No glasses, no hats/head coverings (except religious or medical with a signed statement), no headphones
        • Clothing: everyday clothes; no uniforms or camouflage
        • Quality: high resolution, in focus, even lighting, no filters or digital retouching
        • Recent: taken in the last 6 months
        • Print: matte or glossy photo-quality paper, no damage

        Official source: travel.state.gov. This app is a helper – the passport agency makes the final decision.
